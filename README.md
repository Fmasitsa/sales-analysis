#Beauty Business Performance Analysis


##Project Overview

This project analyzes the sales, expense, services and profitability of a beauty business using data from when the business commenced in April 2024 to August 2026.
The analysis was conducted to understand the financial performance of the business, identify major expense areas, determine which service and periods generates the most revenue, and provides insights that can support better business desicions.


##Business Problem

The business had been recording sales making various purchases and expense, which led me to raise questions about:
- Is the business actually generating a profit?
- How are sales changing over time?
- Which months and quarters perform best?
- Which services generate the most revenue?
- Where is most of the business money being spent?
- How have expenses changed over time?
- Which areas could be improved to increase profitability?
