#Beauty Business Performance Analysis


##Project Overview

This project analyzes the sales, expense, services and profitability of a beauty business using data from when the business commenced in April 2024 to August 2026.
The analysis was conducted to understand the financial performance of the business, identify major expense areas, determine which service and periods generates the most revenue, and provides insights that can support better business desicions.


##Business Problem

The business had been recording sales making various purchases and expense, which led me to raise questions about:
- Is the business actually generating a profit?
- How are sales changing over time?
- Which months and quarters perform best?
- Which services generate the most revenue?
- Where is most of the business money being spent?
- How have expenses changed over time?
- Which areas could be improved to increase profitability?

##Key Findings

###Financial Performance

- **2024:**Ksh 588,400 in sales and a net loss of Ksh 923,016.
- **2025:**Ksh 944,750 in sales and a net profit of Ksh 330,624.
- **2026:**Ksh 188,302 in sales and a net profit of Ksh 58,594 based on the data available.

According to the data we notice that the business moved from a significant loss in 2024 to profitability in 2025 and remained profitable in the available 2026 data.

###Sales Performance

- December recorded the highest monthly sales in 2024 at Ksh 100,700.
- July recorded the highest monthly sales in 2025 at Ksh 98,600.
- May recorded the highest monthly sales in the available 2026 data at approximately Ksh 32,752.
- July recorded the highest cumulative sales across the three year period.

###Expense Analysis

Furniture and Equipment represented approximately 45% of expenditure while Salaries and Employee wages represented approximately 30%.
This highlighted the importance of distinguishing one-time establishment cost from recurring operating expenses when evalauting the business's on going financial performance.

###Service Performance

Tips and Gel-related services were among the strongest revenue generating services in the available sales data.

###Payment Method

Mobile payments represented the majority of recorded sales transactions throughout the analysis period.

##Key Business Insights

The analysis indicates that the business experienced substantial establishment-related expenditure during its first year.
However, financial performance improved significantly in 2025 as sales increased and expenses decreased.
The available 2026 data also shows positive profitability, although the year is incomplete.

The analysis suggests that continued monitoring of recurring expenses, high-revenue services, monthly sales patterns, and profitability will be important for future business performance and decision making.


##Recommendations 

Based on the analysis:

1. Monitor recurring operating expenses regularly.
2. Separate one-time startup costs from recurring business expenses when evaluating ongoing profitability.
3. Continue monitoring high-revenue services such as Tips and Gel-related services.
4. Investigate the factors contributing to strong and weak sales months.
5. Maintain accurate records of mobile and cash payments.
6. Monitor profit margins alongside sales to ensure that revenue growth translates into profitability.
7. Strengthen social media and customer engagement.
8. Encourage clients to follow the business's social media pages and leave reviews.
9. Introduce consistent branding for employees.
10. Use targeted promotions during low sales months.
